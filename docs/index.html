Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Nimly is built around privacy. We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the service — and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

Because Nimly is an anonymous platform, we intentionally collect as little personal information as possible:

  • Account data: A username (no real name required) and a hashed password.
  • Content you post: Text posts, comments, reactions, and direct messages.
  • Device & usage data: Device type, OS version, and app version for debugging purposes only.
  • Camera access: Only when you choose to attach a photo. Images are not stored beyond what you explicitly share.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain the Nimly service
  • To moderate content and enforce community guidelines
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use reputable third-party backend services that comply with applicable data protection regulations. We retain your data only as long as your account is active, or as required by law.

4. User-Generated Content

Content you post on Nimly is visible to other users. Because Nimly is anonymous, posts are not directly linked to your real identity. However, avoid sharing sensitive personal details in public posts.

5. Content Moderation

We review reported content and take action within 24 hours. Content that violates our community guidelines will be removed. Repeat offenders will be permanently banned.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by opening a request at github.com/TheJemv/Nimly/issues.

7. Children's Privacy

Nimly is not intended for users under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Nimly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Privacy questions? Open an issue at github.com/TheJemv/Nimly/issues.
