Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nimly. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our secure vault and anonymous social platform.

2. Data We Collect

To provide the core features of Nimly, we collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Info: We collect your email address for account authentication and security purposes via Supabase.
  • User Content: We store the messages, photos, and posts you upload to enable your private vault and social interactions.
  • Identifiers: We use unique User IDs (UUIDs) to securely associate your data with your account.
  • Diagnostics: We collect anonymous crash logs and performance data to ensure the app remains stable and fast.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly for App Functionality. This includes:

  • Authenticating your identity and securing your account.
  • Enabling the storage and sharing of your encrypted content.
  • Improving app performance and fixing bugs.

Note: We do NOT use your data for tracking, third-party advertising, or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard encryption protocols. We utilize Supabase for backend infrastructure and follow strict security practices to ensure that your private content is only accessible by you and your intended recipients.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell or share your data with data brokers. We only share information with essential service providers (like Supabase) to the extent necessary to provide the app's services.
