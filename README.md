## 🌑 What is Nimly?

Nimly is a **dark-first, anonymous social platform** built for people who want to share honestly without the social pressure of being "on brand." No followers to impress. No algorithm punishing you for being real.

You post. People react. That's it.

Whether you want to vent, confess, share a hot take, or just say something you'd never say with your name attached — Nimly is your space.

<br/>

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🎭 **Full Anonymity** | No real name, no profile photo — every user starts equal |
| 📝 **Posts** | Share text-based thoughts with the Nimly community |
| ❤️ **Likes** | React to posts that resonate with you |
| 💬 **Comments** | Join conversations without revealing who you are |
| 🌙 **Dark UI** | A dark, distraction-free interface built for late-night thoughts |
| 🚫 **Report & Block** | Tools to keep the community safe and respectful |

## 📋 Community Guidelines

Nimly is built on anonymity — but that's not an excuse for cruelty.

By using Nimly, you agree to:
- Not harass, threaten, or abuse other users
- Not post illegal content of any kind
- Accept that content violating our rules will be removed and accounts will be banned
